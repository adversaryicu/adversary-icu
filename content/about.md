Adversary ICU is a publication shell for malware reports, campaign notes, and follow-up field observations. The site is organized as an analyst-facing archive: samples are admitted, examined, and documented in public.

The emphasis is on long-form readability, restrained metadata, and repeatable publishing for technical writing that needs to hold screenshots, code, tables, and IOC material without collapsing into dashboard UI or blog chrome.
